About

Accessible by default, colourful by choice.

I’m a Front-End Engineer with over 20 years’ experience delivering customer-focused digital products across start-ups, large enterprises and FTSE 100 organisations. I’ve worked with well-known brands including ASOS, the British Medical Journal (BMJ), bwin.party and Capita, building and maintaining high-traffic, accessible web platforms used by millions.

My experience spans ecommerce, publishing, healthcare, gaming, finance, telecoms and energy, giving me a broad perspective and the ability to adapt quickly to different industries and teams.

Throughout my career, I’ve balanced hands-on technical delivery with collaboration across UX, QA, analytics and architecture teams. I’ve led front-end work as a sole developer, contributed within large agile squads, and taken on contracting roles for major brands such as Orange, Barclays and Aviva. I tend to stay with organisations long term — six years at bwin.party and nearly seven at BMJ — while also valuing the pace and variety that contracting and start-up environments bring.

I write thoroughly tested code — Jest, Cypress and Playwright are second nature — and build to WCAG AAA, testing with screen readers. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Multimedia Computing from the University of Westminster, and I’ve run stand-ups, retros and knowledge shares, onboarded developers, and picked up whole new stacks like Flutter and Dart with enthusiasm.

Built at ASOS · 2025 — now

Two things shoppers use on every product page — I built them.

During my time at ASOS I built the Filters pull-out menu and the Guided search carousel on the product listing page. Here’s each one, in the wild.

Feature 01

Filters pull-out menu

A slide-in panel that lets shoppers narrow thousands of products by size, colour, brand, length and more — then jump straight back to the results with View items, or reset with Clear all.

  • Sticky header and action bar, with a scrolling list of filters between
  • Refactored from the legacy filter menu and range-based search
  • React & Redux Toolkit, with Jest and Playwright tests, checked with screen readers

Feature 02

Guided search carousel

A horizontal carousel of small image tiles — Long sleeve, Going out, Corset, Halter neck, Lace and more — each labelled with a style, so shoppers can tap straight into a narrower search instead of scrolling the whole category. Next and previous arrows let them move along the row.

  • Swipeable row of image tiles with labels and arrow controls
  • Keyboard and screen-reader friendly
  • Built as a reusable component on the listing page
